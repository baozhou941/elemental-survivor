# V0.1 External Review Consolidation

Date: 2026-07-31  
Baseline: Playable Core V0.1, local-only, no Git repository

## Evidence boundary

Five independent ChatGPT Plus conversations reviewed the same frozen V0.1 evidence. None claimed a human playtest. Engineering and balance reviewers executed parts of the source package; design, art, and player reviewers were read-only. Their advice is external expert input, not acceptance evidence.

## ACCEPT

- Fix five reproducible correctness issues: simultaneous XP loss, XP-cap reward loss, unsafe corner spawn, loop transition scheduling, and hidden frame hitches.
- Keep first upgrade at 60 XP / roughly 34 seconds, but make 70% and 90% progress visibly anticipatory.
- Increase Fire Tornado radius from 72 to 96 while holding damage at 9; instrument hits and zero-hit activations before any further buff.
- Remove unsupported COMMON/RARE labels and rename the radius-only “爆燃” upgrade.
- Record the last damage source and show it at death.
- Adopt restrained Elemental Sigil Minimalism and improve reaction/XP readability within a five-change visual budget.

## MODIFY

- Add exactly one second reaction only as a small V0.2 Build-diversity experiment. It must use a distinct burst behavior and survive measurable 90-second/3-minute tests.
- Add lightweight upgrade-combination hints without exposing every recipe as a tutorial panel.
- Add instrumentation before tuning: XP produced/collected, milestone timestamps, reaction hit efficiency, slow enemy-seconds, hitches.

## REJECT

- Do not lower first-level XP from 60 to 50 yet. The current 34-second evidence is inside the product target and two reviewers explicitly recommend preserving it.
- Do not nerf Fireball from one automated run. Its share is confounded by starting availability and the selected volley upgrade.
- Do not add meta progression, new maps, stores, achievements, or broad content.

## LATER

- Spatial-grid integration and allocation reduction, unless V0.2 measurements show a real bottleneck.
- Weighted upgrade pools, near-miss Build recap, bosses, and complete external art assets.
- Human usability claims. Automation and Plus reviews cannot prove first-time comprehension or replay desire.

## V0.1 answers

1. Is it fun? **Promising but unproven.** The loop works; human repeat-play evidence is absent.
2. Feedback in first 30 seconds? Yes, but progression anticipation is too weak.
3. First upgrade fast enough? 34 seconds meets the 30～45 second target.
4. First qualitative transformation? Fire Tornado appears during the 3-minute run, but its effect is too weak.
5. Strongest Build? Fireball + volley in the single recorded run; not enough evidence for a global conclusion.
6. Trash upgrade? Not proven; “爆燃” is misleadingly named and visually weak.
7. Fair death? Not verifiable because the game does not record or show the last damage source.
8. Is Boss just high HP? No Boss exists in V0.1 by design.
9. Is the screen readable? Generally at the measured peak of 28 enemies, but color hierarchy and reaction readability need work.
10. One more run? The alternative-Build promise exists, but only one implemented reaction makes it fragile.
