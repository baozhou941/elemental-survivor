# Elemental Survivor V0.3 Plan

**Goal:** Add one distinct Fire + Ice reaction and close the highest-value V0.2 control/readability gaps without expanding the content footprint.

## P0 — completed

- Ignore repeated movement `keydown` events so a held key cannot re-enter the input set after pause, level-up, blur, or game-over clears it.
- Aim Fire Tornado's initial orbit toward the nearest live enemy while preserving its V0.2 radius, damage, duration, and stationary orbit behavior.
- Add Thermal Shock: a Fireball striking an already slowed enemy creates a small Fire + Ice burst.
- Give Thermal Shock an independent per-enemy cooldown, exactly-once trigger semantics, AoE damage, activation/hit/zero-hit telemetry, and reset-safe state.
- Keep kills and rewards idempotent when a reaction burst and projectile damage overlap.

## P1 — completed

- Give Fireball, Ice Shard, and Wind Blade distinct circle, diamond, and crescent silhouettes aligned with travel direction.
- Render XP as hollow diamonds to separate rewards from projectiles.
- Add a short directional hurt arc and preserve the final enemy/contact source on the game-over card.
- Add a single unfinished Fire + Ice resonance hint once both weapons are owned.
- Render Thermal Shock as a brief expanding warm/cold ring rather than another persistent orbit.

## P2 — completed

- Extend invariant, input, simulation, configuration, E2E, and balance tests.
- Constrain Node test discovery to `tests/*.test.js` so stale review staging folders cannot duplicate the suite.
- Re-run the deterministic 30-seed balance runner and real three-minute browser journey.

## Acceptance result

- Check suite: passed, 63/63.
- E2E complete journey: passed, 1/1.
- Deterministic simulation: 30/30 runs reached 180 seconds; both reactions were selected in every scripted route.
- Real browser: passed for 180.45 seconds; level 8, 249 kills, 6/6 health.
- Milestones: first kill 1.68 seconds, first XP 4.83 seconds, first level-up 23.43 seconds.
- Fire Tornado: 19 activations, 33 hits, 5 zero-hit activations, 297 damage.
- Thermal Shock: 8 activations, 6 hits, 4 zero-hit activations, 72 damage.
- Average FPS 59.03 with zero recorded hitches; peaks were 16 enemies, 7 projectiles, and 40 particles.
- Screenshot review preserved player/threat/projectile readability. Accumulated XP diamonds are a V0.4 observation item.
- Subjective fun, fairness, and replay desire still require human play evidence.
